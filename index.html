GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

Hello! My name is Kaushik Chakma, and I am a college student with a strong interest in Data Analytics, technology, and data-driven problem-solving. I am fascinated by how data can help us understand situations, discover patterns, identify trends, and make better-informed decisions. To me, data is more than a collection of numbers; when organised and interpreted correctly, it can provide valuable insights and help answer important questions.

My educational journey began at St. Jude School in Diyun, where I studied from LKG through Class VIII. I continued my secondary education at Mahabodhi School, Diyun, for Classes IX and X. After completing secondary school, I attended Higher Secondary School, Miao, for Classes XI and XII. I am currently pursuing my college education at Dibru College, affiliated with Dibrugarh University, where I aim to expand my knowledge and prepare for future professional opportunities.

As an aspiring Data Analytics professional, I am interested in understanding the complete data analysis process, including data collection, data cleaning, data organisation, exploratory data analysis, interpretation, and visualisation. I want to learn how raw information can be converted into meaningful findings that help people and organisations understand problems and make informed decisions.

I am interested in exploring tools and technologies such as Microsoft Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI. These tools can help analysts organise datasets, work with databases, perform calculations, identify patterns, automate repetitive tasks, and present findings through reports and interactive dashboards. My goal is to develop a solid understanding of these technologies through learning, practice, and hands-on projects.

I also recognise the importance of logical reasoning, statistical thinking, attention to detail, and clear communication in the field of Data Analytics. Analysing information is not only about finding numbers or creating charts; it is also about asking the right questions, checking the quality of data, interpreting results carefully, and explaining what those results mean in a clear and understandable way.

I believe that consistent practice and curiosity are essential for personal and professional growth. I look forward to working with real-world datasets, learning from practical challenges, building meaningful projects, and gradually strengthening my analytical abilities. I want to develop the confidence to approach unfamiliar problems, explore different solutions, and learn from every experience.

My long-term goal is to build a career in Data Analytics where I can combine technical knowledge, analytical thinking, and creativity to turn raw data into useful insights. I am committed to continuous learning and improving my abilities step by step so that I can contribute meaningfully to future projects and professional environments.
